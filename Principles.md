# Principles & Values

## Values
1. Individuals and interactions over process and tools  
2. Working software over comprehensive documentation  
3. Customer collaboration over contract negotiation  
4. Responding to change over following a plan  

As set forth in the [Agile Manifesto](https://agilemanifesto.org/), there 4 Values of Agile Development. They are high level statements of priority that establish what Agile practitioners **should value more** when tensions arise between two competing approaches.  

## Principles
You can read the about the [12 Principles behind the Agile Manifesto here](https://agilealliance.org/agile101/12-principles-behind-the-agile-manifesto/).  

The 12 Principles are more specific, actionable guidelines that describe how teams **should behave and operate** in order to live out those values day to day.

There are 12 Principles in Agile Development. Here we look at them and attempt to find a "parallel" principle that would work for Waterfall.

**Principle #1**:  
**Agile:** Our highest priority is to satisfy the customer through the early and continuous delivery of valuable software.  
> Quick Phrase: *Customer is King!*  

**Planned:** Our highest priority is to satisfy the customer by delivering a complete, high-quality product that meets the agreed requirements on schedule.

---

**Principle #2**:  
**Agile:** Welcome changing requirements, even late in development. Agile processes harness change for the customer's competitive advantage.  
> Quick Phrase: *Embrace change*  

**Planned:** Manage requirement changes through formal review and approval processes to maintain scope, cost, and schedule commitments.

---

**Principle #3**:  
**Agile:** Deliver working software frequently, from a couple of weeks to a couple of months, with a preference to the shorter timescale.  
> Quick Phrase: *Continuous Integration*  

**Planned:** Deliver software according to planned milestones, and final delivery date, emphasizing completeness, stability, and integration.

---

**Principle #4**:  
**Agile:** Business people and developers must work together daily throughout the project.  
> Quick Phrase: *Work together*  

**Planned:** Business stakeholders and developers should coordinate through documentation at planned checkpoints to ensure alignment with project goals.

---

**Principle #5**:   
**Agile:** Build projects around motivated individuals. Give them the environment and support they need, and trust them to get the job done.  
> Quick Phrase: *Trust your teammates*  

**Planned:** Build projects around clearly defined roles, responsibilities, and management structures that support successful execution.

---

**Principle #6**:  
**Agile:** The most efficient and effective method of conveying information to and within a development team is face-to-face conversation.  
> Quick Phrase: *Talk to people*  

**Planned:** Clear documentation, specifications, and formal communication channels provide reliable project communication that spans across space and time.

---

**Principle #7**:    
**Agile:** Working software is the primary measure of progress.  
> Quick Phrase: *Measure working Software*   

**Planned:** Progress is measured by the on-time completion of planned deliverables and approved artifacts.

---

**Principle #8**:  
**Agile:** Agile processes promote sustainable development. The sponsors, developers, and users should be able to maintain a constant pace indefinitely.  
> Quick Phrase: *Sustainable working pace*  

**Planned:** Projects should be planned and managed to achieve objectives while maintaining sustainable use of time and resources. All should be able to maintain a constant pace indefinitely.  
> Note: There could be no change in this principle's wording aside from swapping "Planned" for "Agile" since there is no "how" in the Agile statement. However, Waterfall models tend to experience a **Death March** more frequently than Agile methods.  

---

**Principle #9**:  
**Agile:** Continuous attention to technical excellence and good design enhances agility.  

> Quick Phrase: *Develop well today*   

**Planned:** Thorough architecture, design, and engineering practices up-front contributes to a project that requires no rework.  

---

**Principle #10**:  
**Agile:** Simplicity, the art of maximizing the amount of work not done, is essential.  
> Quck Prase: *Avoid needless work*  

**Planned:** Thorough and careful planning should identify and organize all necessary work so only the required work is completed.

---

**Principle #11**:  
**Agile:** The best architectures, requirements, and designs emerge from self-organizing teams.  
> Quick Phrase: *Empower invested teams*  

**Planned:** The best architectures, requirements, and designs result from deliberate planning, expert analysis, and established processes.

---

**Principle #12**:  
**Agile:** At regular intervals, the team reflects on how to become more effective, then tunes and adjusts its behavior accordingly.  
> Quick Phrase: *Always improve*  

**Planned:** Flawless execution of the process should result in the highest efficiency achievable.
